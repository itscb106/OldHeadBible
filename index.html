1 · League Overview

The Old Head Fantasy League is a 12-team competitive fantasy football league for people who have been doing this long enough to know better, yet here we are again. All members are governed by the rules set forth in this document and are expected to know, follow, and respect these by-laws — ignorance of the rules is not a valid excuse, but it is a time-honored tradition. Rules may be amended through the voting process outlined in Section 8.

Trade Rules
  • Trade Deadline: All trades must be completed prior to the start of NFL Week 12. This is 2 weeks before the fantasy playoffs begin, giving all teams a fair and stable roster heading into the postseason.
  • Trade Review Period: 1 day. All league members may cast a veto vote during this window.
  • Trade Vetos: A trade requires 3 veto votes from league members to be overturned. All members are eligible to vote on every trade.
  • Commissioner Veto Authority: The commissioner reserves the right to unilaterally veto any trade deemed to involve collusion or to be grossly imbalanced to the detriment of league integrity. This authority will be exercised responsibly and sparingly.
Eliminated Teams

Any team mathematically eliminated from playoff contention (not eligible for 5th place or better) is locked from making trades or free-agency moves for the remainder of the season.

6 · Conduct

  • All managers are expected to set their lineups each week. Intentionally starting injured or inactive players to tank is considered poor sportsmanship and is subject to commissioner review.
  • Collusion of any kind — including lopsided trades designed to unfairly benefit one team — is prohibited and may result in removal from the league at the commissioner's discretion.
  • The commissioner has final say on disputes not explicitly covered by these by-laws.

8 · Rule Changes & Voting

All rule-change proposals must be submitted to the commissioner prior to Week 1 of the NFL season. Proposals will be compiled and put to a league-wide vote before the season begins. Once rules have been voted on and finalized, no additional changes may be made during the active season. An exception may be granted only if there is a clear consensus among league members that something material was overlooked during the pre-season voting process. All rules must be finalized prior to the start of NFL Week 1.

Voting Process
  • All proposed changes will be put to a league-wide vote conducted via poll in the league group chat.
  • All votes — including roster and scoring changes — require a simple majority (more than 50% of active members) to pass.
  • Votes must be completed within 7 days of the poll being posted.
  • Approved changes take effect the following season unless otherwise specified.

10 · Commissioner & League Office

Commissioner: CB

The commissioner is responsible for managing league settings, resolving disputes, facilitating rule votes, collecting entry fees, and maintaining these by-laws. The commissioner has final authority on all matters not explicitly resolved by these by-laws.

Deputy Commissioner: Oz Kasch

Oz Kasch has been personally appointed by the commissioner to serve as Deputy Commissioner. Oz was selected for this role based on his consistent activity in the league and his deep knowledge of fantasy football. In the event the commissioner is unavailable, conflicted, or unable to render a decision, Oz Kasch shall assume commissioner authority for that matter. Oz Kasch also serves as the designated adjudicator for any dispute in which the commissioner is a directly involved party.
